Independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the Premier League or any club.
Club and competition names are used descriptively. No logos or crests are used.

## Data and sources
- **Fixtures, kick-offs, results:** the published 2026/27 list, refreshed by `build_atlas.py` from the public Fantasy Premier League feed. Check licensing with Football DataCo (football-dataco.com) before any commercial use.
- **Stadium coordinates:** approximate, refined with OpenStreetMap data via `fetch_stadiums.py` (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.
- **Population:** indicative values built into the page. Exact values come from the ONS LSOA 2021 population-weighted centroids and mid-year population estimates. Check each dataset's licence statement and add the attribution it asks for (ONS data is generally published under the Open Government Licence).
- **Road times:** OSRM routing over OpenStreetMap data (ODbL).
- **Satellite imagery** (`odyssey-satellite.html`): the provider's attribution is shown on the map (Esri, MapTiler or Mapbox). Follow that provider's terms.
- **Map library:** MapLibre GL JS (BSD-3-Clause).
- **Sun position:** NOAA-style solar position formulas, accurate to about half a degree.
